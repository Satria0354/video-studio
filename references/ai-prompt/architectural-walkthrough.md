# Architectural Walkthrough

Interior/exterior real-estate or design showcase footage.

## Vocabulary
- **Camera:** smooth forward glide through a space, slow reveal turning a corner, steady
  orbit around an exterior
- **Setting:** modern minimalist interior, luxury open-plan kitchen, exterior facade at
  golden hour, landscaped garden
- **Lighting:** natural daylight through large windows, warm evening interior lighting,
  golden hour exterior

## Prompt template
```
Architectural walkthrough, smooth [camera movement] through [space description],
[lighting], [material/design details], clean modern real-estate photography style,
high detail
```

## Example
"Architectural walkthrough, smooth forward glide through a modern open-plan living room,
natural daylight through floor-to-ceiling windows, polished concrete floors and warm wood
accents, clean modern real-estate photography style, high detail."

## Tips
- Smooth, slow, steady camera movement is essential to this genre — explicitly ask for
  "smooth" and "steady" since architectural content reads as unprofessional with any
  handheld shake.
- Mention specific materials (concrete, wood, marble, glass) — they affect how the model
  renders light and reflection a lot.
- Keep people out of the shot (or keep them minimal/background) unless the user specifically
  wants a lifestyle element — empty, well-lit spaces are far more reliable to generate well.
