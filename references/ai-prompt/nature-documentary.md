# Nature Documentary

Wildlife and landscape footage in the style of a nature documentary.

## Vocabulary
- **Subjects:** wildlife (real species, described naturally), landscapes, weather phenomena,
  macro nature detail (water droplets, insects, plants)
- **Camera:** slow telephoto tracking shot, steady wide landscape shot, macro close-up
- **Lighting:** golden hour, harsh midday, dramatic storm light, underwater light shafts

## Prompt template
```
Nature documentary style, [animal/landscape subject] [natural behavior/action], in
[habitat], [lighting/weather], [camera movement], realistic, high detail, David
Attenborough-style cinematography
```

## Example
"Nature documentary style, a bald eagle gliding low over a river surface, in a misty
mountain valley, soft golden morning light, slow telephoto tracking shot, realistic,
high detail."

## Tips
- Describe natural, species-typical behavior (gliding, grazing, stalking, drinking) rather
  than anthropomorphized action — it renders more convincingly and reads more authentically.
- This genre benefits a lot from explicit habitat detail (river, savanna, tundra, coral
  reef) since it grounds the lighting and color palette.
- Real animal anatomy is hard for video models to keep fully consistent during complex
  motion (e.g. a running four-legged animal) — simpler behaviors (standing, grazing,
  gliding, swimming) tend to render more reliably than fast locomotion.
