# Horror / Thriller

Tension, dread, suspense — atmosphere-driven rather than graphic.

## Vocabulary
- **Setting:** dark abandoned hallway, foggy forest at night, flickering hospital corridor,
  empty house at night
- **Lighting:** harsh single flashlight beam, flickering fluorescent light, deep shadow with
  thin light source, cold blue moonlight
- **Camera:** slow creeping push-in, handheld unsteady movement, static shot holding on an
  empty space too long

## Prompt template
```
Horror atmosphere, [setting], [lighting], [camera movement], tense and unsettling mood,
[specific unsettling detail], dark color grade
```

## Example
"Horror atmosphere, a long abandoned hospital hallway, flickering fluorescent lights casting
moving shadows, slow creeping push-in down the corridor, tense and unsettling mood, dark
desaturated color grade."

## Tips
- Suggestion and absence (empty space, a door slightly ajar, a shadow that shouldn't be
  there) reads as scarier and renders more reliably than an explicit monster/gore reveal.
- Avoid requesting graphic violence or gore — keep this genre to atmosphere and suspense,
  which is both safer and more achievable with current video models.
- A static shot held a beat too long on an empty space is a classic, very achievable
  horror-pacing technique worth suggesting to the user.
