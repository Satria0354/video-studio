# Cyberpunk / Sci-Fi

Neon-lit futuristic cities, advanced tech, dystopian or high-tech atmosphere.

## Vocabulary
- **Setting:** rain-slicked neon streets, elevated walkways between skyscrapers, holographic
  billboards, dense vertical cityscape, underground tech market
- **Lighting/color:** neon pink/cyan/purple palette, volumetric fog with light shafts,
  wet-reflection on pavement
- **Camera:** slow drone descent into the city, tracking shot along an elevated walkway,
  low-angle hero shot with neon signage behind

## Prompt template
```
Cyberpunk cityscape, [subject/action or "no subject, establishing shot"], [setting detail],
neon [color] lighting, volumetric fog, wet reflective surfaces, [camera movement],
futuristic, highly detailed
```

## Example
"Cyberpunk cityscape establishing shot, towering skyscrapers connected by elevated glowing
walkways, dense neon pink and cyan signage, volumetric fog with light shafts, wet reflective
streets below, slow drone descent revealing the scale of the city, highly detailed."

## Tips
- This genre leans heavily on environment and lighting rather than character action — strong
  establishing/atmosphere shots are very achievable even with a simple prompt.
- If including a character, keep the design original (see Copyright section) — cyberpunk
  character archetypes are common across many existing properties, so describe specifics
  (clothing texture, hair, accessories) rather than naming any existing character or IP.
- "Volumetric fog" and "wet reflective surfaces" are high-value keywords for this look —
  they do a lot of the atmosphere work on their own.
