# Fantasy / Epic

Magic, mythical creatures, medieval or otherworldly grand-scale scenes.

## Vocabulary
- **Setting:** ancient stone ruins, floating islands, enchanted forest, dragon's lair,
  towering castle at dusk
- **Elements:** glowing magic particles, swirling energy, mythical creature (original design),
  dramatic weather (storm clouds, mist)
- **Camera:** sweeping crane shot, low-angle hero shot, slow orbit revealing scale
- **Lighting:** dramatic god rays through clouds, torch/firelight, moonlit blue tones

## Prompt template
```
Epic fantasy scene, [subject/creature, original design], [single action], in [setting],
[weather/atmosphere], [camera movement], dramatic lighting with god rays, grand scale,
highly detailed
```

## Example
"Epic fantasy scene, a massive stone golem slowly turning its head toward the camera, in
ancient overgrown ruins, mist rolling across the ground, low-angle shot emphasizing scale,
dramatic god rays breaking through the canopy, highly detailed."

## Tips
- Scale is the main thing to sell in this genre — use explicit scale cues (low angle,
  tiny human figure for comparison, wide shot showing full height) rather than just saying
  "huge" or "epic."
- Keep creature/character designs original rather than naming existing franchises.
- A single slow, weighty action (turning, rising, roaring) reads better than fast combat
  choreography, which most models struggle to render coherently.
