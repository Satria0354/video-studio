# Talking Avatar

A character or person appears to speak to camera — common for explainer/narration content.

## Vocabulary
- **Framing:** head-and-shoulders medium shot, direct eye contact with camera
- **Setting:** simple studio background, blurred office background, branded backdrop
- **Expression cues:** warm smile, neutral professional expression, animated hand gestures

## Prompt template
```
[Realistic / 3D animated / 2D animated] [person/character description] speaking directly to
camera, head-and-shoulders medium shot, [setting], natural expression and subtle head
movement, [lighting]
```

## Example
"Realistic medium shot of a friendly presenter speaking directly to camera, simple blurred
office background, natural expression with subtle head movement, soft even studio lighting."

## Tips
- Most general video-gen tools do **not** reliably lip-sync to a specific audio track —
  that needs a dedicated lip-sync/avatar tool (e.g. HeyGen, D-ID, Runway's lip-sync features
  where available). If exact lip-sync to pre-recorded audio is the goal, say so explicitly
  and flag that a specialized tool is likely needed rather than a general video-gen prompt.
- For a silent/ambient talking-head shot (no sync needed), keep the action simple: natural
  subtle head movement and expression, not specific words.
- If the user needs the same presenter across multiple clips, recommend image-to-video from
  one fixed reference image rather than re-describing the person from scratch each time.
