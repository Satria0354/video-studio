# Drone / Aerial

Sweeping landscape and cityscape shots, establishing shots, flyovers.

## Vocabulary
- **Movement:** slow ascend, descend, forward flythrough, orbit, reveal (rising over an
  obstacle to reveal a vista)
- **Subjects:** coastline, mountain range, city skyline, forest canopy, river delta, highway
- **Lighting/time:** sunrise mist, golden hour, blue hour city lights, overcast moody
- **Style:** hyper-realistic drone cinematography, documentary aerial footage

## Prompt template
```
Aerial drone shot, [movement], over [landscape/city feature], [time of day/weather],
[lighting], wide dynamic range, cinematic documentary style
```

## Example
"Aerial drone shot, slow forward flythrough above a misty mountain valley at sunrise,
golden light breaking through fog, cinematic documentary style, wide dynamic range."

## Tips
- Aerial shots are one of the most reliable genres for current video models — few moving
  foreground subjects, mostly camera motion over static terrain.
- Avoid combining drone movement with a detailed foreground human action — pick one focus.
- "Reveal" shots (rising over a ridge/building to show what's beyond) read very well to
  most models when described explicitly as a reveal.
