# Cinematic Live-Action

Realistic, narrative, human-centered footage — the look of a drama, indie film, or commercial with real actors.

## Vocabulary to draw on
- **Framing:** wide shot, medium shot, close-up, over-the-shoulder, low-angle, high-angle
- **Camera movement:** slow push-in, dolly, handheld (subtle/shaky), steadicam glide, rack focus
- **Lighting:** golden hour, blue hour, soft window light, practical lighting, high-contrast chiaroscuro
- **Lens feel:** shallow depth of field, anamorphic flare, 35mm film grain
- **Color grade:** warm and desaturated, teal-and-orange, muted naturalistic

## Prompt template
```
[Framing] of [person, age/look] [single specific action] in [setting], [camera movement],
[lighting], shallow depth of field, [color grade], cinematic, realistic
```

## Example
"Medium close-up of a middle-aged man sitting at a kitchen table, slowly turning a coffee
mug in his hands, soft morning window light, shallow depth of field, slow push-in,
warm desaturated color grade, cinematic, realistic."

## Tips
- Realistic human faces and hands are the hardest thing for most video models — keep actions
  simple (sitting, turning, walking, looking) rather than complex manipulation (typing,
  playing an instrument in detail).
- Specifying a lens/depth-of-field cue noticeably improves the "real camera" feel.
- Avoid asking for dialogue lip-sync unless the tool explicitly supports it — most don't.
