# Vintage / Retro Film

Nostalgic old-footage look — VHS, Super 8, 16mm film, old home-video aesthetic.

## Vocabulary
- **Style descriptors:** VHS footage look, Super 8mm film, 16mm film grain, old home video,
  faded color film stock
- **Artifacts:** film grain, light leaks, slight chromatic aberration, scan lines
  (VHS-specific), vignette corners, washed-out color
- **Era cues:** 1970s color palette, 1980s VHS, 1990s camcorder

## Prompt template
```
[Era/format] footage style, [subject] [single action], in [setting], film grain, [specific
artifacts], [color treatment], nostalgic, slightly degraded quality
```

## Example
"1980s VHS home-video footage style, a family dancing in a living room during a birthday
party, warm tungsten lighting, visible scan lines and light leaks, washed-out warm color
treatment, nostalgic, slightly degraded quality."

## Tips
- Naming the specific era/format (VHS vs Super 8 vs 16mm) changes the result a lot — they
  have distinct, recognizable signatures, so pick one rather than saying "vintage" alone.
- This look pairs well with handheld, slightly unstable camera motion — mention it if
  relevant to the mood.
- Great for nostalgic/emotional content; less suited to crisp product or tech content.
