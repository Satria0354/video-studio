# Product Commercial

Hero shots and showcase footage for a product — the look of an ad or e-commerce video.

## Vocabulary
- **Shot types:** 360 orbit, macro close-up on detail/texture, slow reveal rise, hero beauty shot
- **Setting:** clean studio backdrop, lifestyle context (in-use), minimal gradient background
- **Lighting:** soft studio lighting, rim light to separate product from background,
  dramatic single-source spotlight

## Prompt template
```
Product commercial shot, [product description], [camera move] on a [background type],
[lighting], [surface/reflection detail], clean high-end advertising look
```

## Example
"Product commercial shot, a matte black wireless speaker, slow 360 orbit on a minimal
gray gradient background, soft studio lighting with a rim light edge, subtle reflection
on a glossy surface below, clean high-end advertising look."

## Tips
- If the user has an actual product photo, this is a strong candidate for image-to-video
  (motion/camera only in the prompt) rather than text-to-video, since exact product
  appearance (logo, exact shape) is hard to get right from text alone.
- Orbit and slow push-in camera moves are the most reliable for product shots; avoid
  asking the product itself to "transform" or move in physically implausible ways unless
  that's explicitly the creative concept.
- Mention surface/material explicitly (matte, glossy, brushed metal, fabric) — it changes
  how lighting reads a lot.
